Senior Research Scientist & Professor

Building AI systems
we can trust.

I’m Maxime Cordy, a Senior Research Scientist and Professor at SnT - The Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust, University of Luxembourg. I lead impact-driven research at the intersection of trustworthy AI, software engineering and industry applications. I am your trusted partner to turn ambitious ideas into dependable real-world systems.

As deputy head of the Security, Reasoning and Validation group, I lead a team working on AI quality, security and innovative applications. I collaborate with companies and public institutions across finance, energy, cybersecurity, manufacturing, official statistics and space—from fundamental research to deployed prototypes and spin-offs.
